# INC-2026-0322: Stale balances on merchant settlement portal

## Summary
The merchant settlement portal showed settlement figures up to 3 hours old because its read replica was lagging.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV3
- **Date:** 2026-03-22, 08:00 to 11:45 (Asia/Colombo), 225 minutes
- **Affected services:** Merchant Settlement Portal (`merchant-settlement-portal`), Ledger Database Cluster (`ledger-db`)
- **Customer impact:** About 420 customers affected. No transactions failed.
- **Incident commander:** Tharindu Silva

## Detection
Merchant complaints at 09:30.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Replica lag starts growing after a large index rebuild. |
| 09:30 | Merchants report old figures. |
| 11:45 | Replica caught up. |

## Root Cause
An index rebuild on the primary generated more write-ahead log than the reporting replica could apply.

## Contributing Factors
- No alert on replica lag for the reporting replica.

## Resolution
Waited for catch-up; portal showed a staleness banner from 10:00.
