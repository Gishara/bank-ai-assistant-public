# INC-2026-0412: Major card outage on Sinhala and Tamil New Year eve

## Summary
Card authorization was severely degraded for 71 minutes on the busiest shopping day of the year. ATM withdrawals were also affected because ATM PIN checks use the same HSM path.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV1
- **Date:** 2026-04-12, 16:22 to 17:33 (Asia/Colombo), 71 minutes
- **Affected services:** Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`), HSM Gateway (`hsm-gateway`)
- **Customer impact:** 39,600 transactions failed or timed out; about 24,800 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Paging alert at 16:23. Customer complaints on social media from 16:30.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 16:22 | Authorization volume at 3.4x normal; latency rising. |
| 16:23 | HSM utilization page. |
| 16:31 | SEV1 declared. CTO and communications team notified. |
| 16:48 | Pool raised from 192 to 256; partial recovery. |
| 17:05 | Second HSM appliance brought online from standby. |
| 17:33 | Full recovery. |

## Root Cause
The HSM cluster ran out of available sessions under festival load. Raising the session ceiling alone was not enough because the active appliances were near their cryptographic throughput limit; queued PIN translations and cryptogram checks waited beyond the authorization timeout.

## Contributing Factors
- The standby HSM appliance was not part of the active pool.
- Demand-based session scaling, first raised in November 2025, was still not built.
- Seasonal capacity review used last year's peak, not projected growth.

## Resolution
Session ceiling raised to 256 and the standby appliance added to the active pool.
