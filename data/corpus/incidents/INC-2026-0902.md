# INC-2026-0902: Mobile app crash on Android 15 devices

## Summary
Version 5.0.1 of the mobile app crashed at launch on some Android 15 devices.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV3
- **Date:** 2026-09-02, 10:00 to 16:30 (Asia/Colombo), 390 minutes
- **Affected services:** Mobile Banking API (`mobile-banking-api`)
- **Customer impact:** About 8,800 customers affected. No transactions failed.
- **Incident commander:** Nuwan Bandara

## Detection
Crash reporting alert at 10:20.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 10:00 | Staged rollout reaches 20%. |
| 10:20 | Crash rate alert. |
| 10:35 | Rollout halted. |
| 16:30 | Hotfix 5.0.2 released. |

## Root Cause
A biometric prompt library call that changed behaviour on Android 15 threw an unhandled exception during app start.

## Contributing Factors
- Device test farm lacked Android 15 devices from two manufacturers.

## Resolution
Hotfix released; rollout resumed.
