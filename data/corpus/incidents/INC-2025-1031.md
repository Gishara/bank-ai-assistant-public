# INC-2025-1031: Morning card and transfer failures after month-end settlement

## Summary
Card authorizations and fund transfers timed out for 63 minutes on the first business morning after month-end.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2025-10-31, 06:35 to 07:38 (Asia/Colombo), 63 minutes
- **Affected services:** End-of-Day Settlement Batch (`eod-settlement-batch`), Ledger Database Cluster (`ledger-db`), Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`), Core Banking Adapter (`core-banking-adapter`)
- **Customer impact:** 7,300 transactions failed or timed out; about 5,900 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Batch overrun alert at 06:30; authorization timeout alert at 06:38.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 01:00 | Month-end settlement batch starts, 2.2x normal posting volume. |
| 06:30 | Batch still running at end of its window. |
| 06:38 | Authorization timeouts begin as customers start their day. |
| 07:02 | DBA identifies blocking sessions from the batch holding locks on settlement and fee accounts. |
| 07:20 | Batch paused at a checkpoint. |
| 07:38 | Latency normal; batch resumed at 09:00 under supervision. |

## Root Cause
Month-end volume pushed the settlement batch past its window. It posts in large transactions that hold row locks on shared settlement and fee accounts for minutes at a time. Live authorizations and transfers that debit or credit the same accounts blocked on those locks until their timeouts expired.

## Contributing Factors
- Batch commits every 50,000 postings, so each transaction holds locks for a long time.
- No automatic pause when the window is exceeded.
- The review of commit size after INC-2025-0815 concluded no change was needed.

## Resolution
Batch paused manually and resumed after the morning peak.
