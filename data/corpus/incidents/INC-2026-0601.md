# INC-2026-0601: Phishing campaign impersonating the Bank

## Summary
An SMS phishing campaign directed customers to a fake login page. 63 customers entered credentials before the site was taken down.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2026-06-01, 07:30 to 18:00 (Asia/Colombo), 630 minutes
- **Affected services:** Identity Service (`identity-service`)
- **Customer impact:** About 63 customers affected. No transactions failed.
- **Incident commander:** Chamari Rathnayake

## Detection
Customer reports and brand monitoring alert at 08:10.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 07:30 | First phishing SMS observed. |
| 08:10 | Brand monitoring alert. |
| 10:45 | Takedown request accepted by hosting provider. |
| 18:00 | All affected customers contacted and credentials reset. |

## Root Cause
External criminal campaign. No Bank system was compromised.

## Contributing Factors
- Lookalike domain registered two days before the campaign.

## Resolution
Site taken down, affected accounts secured, customer advisory published.
