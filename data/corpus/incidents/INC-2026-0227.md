# INC-2026-0227: Incorrect fees charged on QR payments

## Summary
A deployment applied a merchant fee table to customer QR payments, charging customers LKR 25 per payment for 2 hours 20 minutes.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV3
- **Date:** 2026-02-27, 11:00 to 13:20 (Asia/Colombo), 140 minutes
- **Affected services:** QR Payments Service (`qr-payments-service`)
- **Customer impact:** About 2,300 customers affected. No transactions failed.
- **Incident commander:** Sanduni Abeysekara

## Detection
Customer complaints through the call center.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 11:00 | Release 4.12 of the QR payments service deployed. |
| 12:30 | Call center reports unexpected fees. |
| 13:05 | Release rolled back. |
| 13:20 | Fee charging stops. Refunds scheduled. |

## Root Cause
A configuration key for the fee table was renamed in the release, and the service fell back to the merchant fee table for customer payments. The change was approved as low risk and the fee scenarios were not part of the regression suite.

## Contributing Factors
- Change classified as standard although it touched pricing.
- No production smoke test for fees.

## Resolution
Rollback and automatic refund of LKR 57,500 to 2,300 customers.
