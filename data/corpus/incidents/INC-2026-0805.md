# INC-2026-0805: Payment switch connector unable to resolve partner host

## Summary
Interbank transfers failed for 27 minutes after an internal DNS server migration.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2026-08-05, 22:10 to 22:37 (Asia/Colombo), 27 minutes
- **Affected services:** National Payment Switch Connector (`payment-switch-connector`)
- **Customer impact:** 980 transactions failed or timed out; about 870 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Synthetic transfer monitor at 22:12.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 22:00 | Planned migration of internal DNS resolvers. |
| 22:10 | Connector nodes fail to resolve the switch endpoint hostname. |
| 22:25 | Missing conditional forwarder for the switch operator's zone identified. |
| 22:37 | Forwarder added; recovery. |

## Root Cause
The new DNS resolvers were built without the conditional forwarder for the switch operator's private zone, so connector nodes could not resolve the partner hostname once their cached record expired.

## Contributing Factors
- Migration plan did not list partner zones.
- Change was scheduled during transfer hours.

## Resolution
Conditional forwarder added to new resolvers.
