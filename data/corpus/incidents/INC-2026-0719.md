# INC-2026-0719: Instant transfer failures after switch operator CA change

## Summary
Interbank transfers failed for 41 minutes after the switch operator rotated its server certificate to a new intermediate CA.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2026-07-19, 06:00 to 06:41 (Asia/Colombo), 41 minutes
- **Affected services:** National Payment Switch Connector (`payment-switch-connector`)
- **Customer impact:** 2,890 transactions failed or timed out; about 2,400 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Synthetic transfer monitor at 06:02.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 06:00 | Switch operator completes scheduled certificate change. |
| 06:02 | Synthetic monitor fails with 'unable to get local issuer certificate'. |
| 06:15 | Switch Integration on-call identifies missing intermediate CA. |
| 06:35 | Trust store updated on connector nodes. |
| 06:41 | Recovery. |

## Root Cause
The switch operator moved to a new intermediate certificate authority. The Bank's connector trust store did not contain that intermediate, so the Bank could no longer verify the switch's certificate and closed each connection. The operator had announced the change by circular five weeks earlier.

## Contributing Factors
- The operator circular was received by Compliance but not routed to Switch Integration.
- Trust store updates require a deployment rather than a configuration reload.

## Resolution
Trust store updated with the new intermediate CA.
