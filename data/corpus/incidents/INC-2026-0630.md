# INC-2026-0630: Half-year close settlement causes payment outage

## Summary
Card, transfer and QR payments failed or were slow for 88 minutes after the half-year close settlement ran long.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV1
- **Date:** 2026-06-30, 06:50 to 08:18 (Asia/Colombo), 88 minutes
- **Affected services:** End-of-Day Settlement Batch (`eod-settlement-batch`), Ledger Database Cluster (`ledger-db`), Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`), National Payment Switch Connector (`payment-switch-connector`)
- **Customer impact:** 15,200 transactions failed or timed out; about 12,100 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Authorization timeout alert at 06:52.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 00:30 | Half-year close batch starts with interest capitalization added. |
| 06:30 | Batch exceeds window. |
| 06:52 | Timeouts across card and transfer channels. |
| 07:15 | SEV1 declared. |
| 07:40 | Interest capitalization step found holding locks on all customer accounts touched in its run. |
| 08:05 | Step terminated; rollback completes at 08:12. |
| 08:18 | Recovery. |

## Root Cause
An interest capitalization step, added to the batch for half-year close, updated customer account balances in one long-running transaction. The batch overran into business hours, and every payment touching an account in that step's range waited on its row locks until the channel timeout. This is the same lock-holding pattern as the October and January incidents, introduced again by a new step.

## Contributing Factors
- The automatic pause at window end was still not delivered.
- New batch steps are not reviewed against the commit-size standard set in December.
- The rollback took 7 minutes because of the transaction size.

## Resolution
Interest step terminated and rerun at 22:00.
