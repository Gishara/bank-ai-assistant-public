# INC-2026-0828: Contactless payment declines at supermarket chain

## Summary
Contactless transactions were declined for 14 minutes, mainly at one supermarket chain running a lunchtime promotion.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV3
- **Date:** 2026-08-28, 12:10 to 12:24 (Asia/Colombo), 14 minutes
- **Affected services:** Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`), HSM Gateway (`hsm-gateway`)
- **Customer impact:** 2,310 transactions failed or timed out; about 1,980 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Dilani Fernando

## Detection
Utilization alert at 12:11; merchant call at 12:15.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 12:10 | Contactless cryptogram verification latency rises. |
| 12:11 | HSM utilization alert. |
| 12:16 | On-call finds autoscaling capped at 160 sessions by a default configuration value. |
| 12:24 | Cap raised to 256; recovery. |

## Root Cause
The new demand-based HSM session scaler shipped in July with its maximum left at a default of 160, below the static 256 used before. When a promotion drove a short burst of contactless traffic, the scaler hit that cap, sessions ran out, and cryptogram validation timed out.

## Contributing Factors
- Release checklist for the scaler did not compare new limits with the previous static configuration.
- Contactless transactions use a shorter 2 second timeout.

## Resolution
Scaler maximum set to 320.
