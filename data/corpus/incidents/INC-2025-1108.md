# INC-2025-1108: Intermittent card declines on Saturday evening

## Summary
POS and e-commerce card transactions were intermittently declined for 47 minutes on a Saturday evening with higher than normal retail activity ahead of the Deepavali holiday.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2025-11-08, 18:40 to 19:27 (Asia/Colombo), 47 minutes
- **Affected services:** Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`), HSM Gateway (`hsm-gateway`)
- **Customer impact:** 18,420 transactions failed or timed out; about 11,250 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Alert on HSM session utilization fired at 18:43 (above 80%), followed by the decline-rate alert at 18:46.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 18:40 | Authorization latency p95 increases from 180 ms to 2.6 s. |
| 18:43 | HSM session utilization alert fires at 92%. |
| 18:52 | Incident declared SEV2; bridge opened. |
| 19:05 | Engineers confirm all 96 sessions occupied; requests timing out. |
| 19:18 | Pool raised to 128 through the runtime setting added after INC-2024-1129. |
| 19:27 | Latency and decline rate return to normal. |

## Root Cause
Traffic grew faster than the static session allowance. The 96 connections to the HSM cluster were all busy, so PIN verification calls from the card authorization service could not acquire a connection within the 3 second budget and the transaction was declined.

## Contributing Factors
- The utilization alert existed but nobody was paged until the decline-rate alert, because it was configured as a warning.
- Capacity plan for the festive season was not shared with Cryptography Services.
- Pool size is still a fixed number rather than scaling with demand.

## Resolution
Session pool raised to 128 at runtime. No restart required.
