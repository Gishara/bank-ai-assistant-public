# INC-2026-0306: QR payments rejected by acquiring partner

## Summary
Customers could not complete merchant QR payments for 58 minutes during the morning peak.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2026-03-06, 09:15 to 10:13 (Asia/Colombo), 58 minutes
- **Affected services:** QR Payments Service (`qr-payments-service`), National Payment Switch Connector (`payment-switch-connector`)
- **Customer impact:** 4,120 transactions failed or timed out; about 3,700 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Sanduni Abeysekara

## Detection
Customer support ticket spike at 09:25; error-rate alert at 09:28.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 09:15 | QR payment confirmations start failing with 'secure channel error'. |
| 09:28 | Error-rate alert fires. |
| 09:40 | Partner confirms the Bank's certificate for the QR acquirer API has expired. |
| 10:05 | Renewed certificate deployed. |
| 10:13 | Recovery confirmed. |

## Root Cause
The client certificate used to authenticate the QR payments service to the acquirer's API had lapsed. It was issued by a different internal CA than the switch certificate and was not in the new expiry-alerting inventory, so no warning was raised.

## Contributing Factors
- The certificate inventory started in December only covered certificates managed by the PKI team.
- The QR service team renewed certificates manually every two years.

## Resolution
Certificate renewed and added to the central inventory.
