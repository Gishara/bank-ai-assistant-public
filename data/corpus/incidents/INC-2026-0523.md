# INC-2026-0523: Card payments down during fraud provider outage

## Summary
All card-not-present and most POS authorizations failed for 52 minutes during a regional outage at the fraud scoring provider.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV1
- **Date:** 2026-05-23, 10:14 to 11:06 (Asia/Colombo), 52 minutes
- **Affected services:** Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`), Fraud Scoring Client (`fraud-scoring-client`)
- **Customer impact:** 21,400 transactions failed or timed out; about 16,900 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Authorization failure alert at 10:15.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 10:14 | VeriScore returns connection errors from its Singapore region. |
| 10:15 | Authorization failures above 60%. |
| 10:22 | SEV1 declared. |
| 10:30 | Circuit breaker found deployed but disabled by a feature flag pending testing. |
| 10:51 | Flag enabled after emergency approval; fallback to local rules. |
| 11:06 | Recovery. |

## Root Cause
Card authorization depends on a live response from the external fraud provider. The circuit breaker and local-rules fallback from INC-2026-0119 had been deployed but were switched off while awaiting fraud model validation, so when the provider's region failed, authorizations failed with it.

## Contributing Factors
- Fallback rollout was blocked on a validation task with no owner.
- The provider status page did not report the outage for 25 minutes.

## Resolution
Fallback flag enabled; local rules used until the provider recovered at 13:40.
