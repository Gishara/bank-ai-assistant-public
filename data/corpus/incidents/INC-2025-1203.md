# INC-2025-1203: Interbank instant transfers failing

## Summary
All outgoing and incoming interbank instant transfers failed for 95 minutes after midnight.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV1
- **Date:** 2025-12-03, 00:01 to 01:36 (Asia/Colombo), 95 minutes
- **Affected services:** National Payment Switch Connector (`payment-switch-connector`)
- **Customer impact:** 6,240 transactions failed or timed out; about 5,100 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Synthetic transfer check failed at 00:06. Night shift operator escalated at 00:20.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 00:01 | Handshake failures begin on the national switch link. |
| 00:06 | Synthetic transfer monitor fails. |
| 00:20 | Escalated to Switch Integration on-call. |
| 00:52 | Logs show the Bank's client certificate expired at 00:00. |
| 01:20 | Emergency certificate issued by PKI team. |
| 01:36 | Link re-established; queued transfers replayed. |

## Root Cause
The mutual TLS client certificate presented by the Bank to the national payment switch reached its expiry date at midnight. The switch rejected every handshake, so no transfer messages could be exchanged.

## Contributing Factors
- The certificate was tracked in a spreadsheet owned by an engineer who had left the Bank.
- Expiry reminders went to a shared mailbox that was no longer monitored.
- The night shift runbook did not cover TLS failures.

## Resolution
New certificate issued and deployed; 1,850 queued transfers replayed from the outbound queue.
