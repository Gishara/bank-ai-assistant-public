# INC-2025-0815: Slow card authorizations after settlement overrun

## Summary
The settlement batch ran 25 minutes past its window and early-morning card transactions were slow.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV3
- **Date:** 2025-08-15, 06:40 to 07:05 (Asia/Colombo), 25 minutes
- **Affected services:** End-of-Day Settlement Batch (`eod-settlement-batch`), Ledger Database Cluster (`ledger-db`), Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`)
- **Customer impact:** 870 transactions failed or timed out; about 760 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Tharindu Silva

## Detection
Batch overrun alert at 06:30.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 06:30 | Settlement batch still running past 06:30 window end. |
| 06:40 | Authorization latency rises. |
| 07:05 | Batch completes; latency recovers. |

## Root Cause
The settlement batch was still updating merchant settlement accounts when morning card traffic began, and authorizations touching those accounts waited on row locks.

## Contributing Factors
- Higher than usual transaction volume after a long weekend.

## Resolution
Waited for batch completion.
