# INC-2026-0214: Card payment slowdowns and declines on Valentine's Day

## Summary
E-commerce and restaurant POS transactions experienced slow responses and declines for 32 minutes.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2026-02-14, 20:05 to 20:37 (Asia/Colombo), 32 minutes
- **Affected services:** Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`), HSM Gateway (`hsm-gateway`)
- **Customer impact:** 11,900 transactions failed or timed out; about 8,030 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Paging alert on HSM utilization at 20:07.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 20:05 | Card-not-present volume reaches 2.8x weekday average. |
| 20:07 | HSM utilization page received by Cryptography Services on-call. |
| 20:15 | SEV2 declared. Pool already at 128, the configured maximum. |
| 20:24 | Emergency change approved to raise the maximum to 192. |
| 20:37 | Recovery confirmed. |

## Root Cause
Demand exceeded the ceiling of 128 HSM sessions. Requests for cryptographic verification queued behind busy sessions, authorization calls hit their 3 second timeout, and the card authorization service declined them. The demand-based scaling action from INC-2025-1108 had not been delivered.

## Contributing Factors
- Raising the ceiling required an emergency change approval, which took 9 minutes.
- Scaling work was deprioritized in January for the tokenization launch.

## Resolution
Ceiling raised to 192 under emergency change CHG-26-0388.
