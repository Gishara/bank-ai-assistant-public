# INC-2026-0119: Card authorization timeouts caused by slow fraud scoring

## Summary
Card authorizations timed out for 36 minutes while the external fraud scoring provider was slow.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2026-01-19, 14:02 to 14:38 (Asia/Colombo), 36 minutes
- **Affected services:** Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`), Fraud Scoring Client (`fraud-scoring-client`)
- **Customer impact:** 6,700 transactions failed or timed out; about 5,200 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Authorization latency alert at 14:05.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 14:02 | VeriScore p99 latency rises from 300 ms to 8 s. |
| 14:05 | Authorization latency alert. |
| 14:12 | Thread pool in card-auth-service exhausted. |
| 14:25 | Fraud Analytics lead approves bypass with rules-only scoring. |
| 14:38 | Recovery after bypass flag enabled. |

## Root Cause
The card authorization service calls the external fraud scoring API synchronously and waits up to 10 seconds for a score. When the provider slowed down, authorization threads were held waiting, the thread pool was exhausted, and new transactions were rejected even though the Bank's own systems were healthy.

## Contributing Factors
- No timeout shorter than the overall authorization budget.
- No automatic fallback to the Bank's local rules engine.
- Bypass required a manual approval from Fraud Analytics.

## Resolution
Manual bypass to rules-only scoring until the provider recovered.
