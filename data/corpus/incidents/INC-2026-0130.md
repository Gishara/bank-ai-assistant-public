# INC-2026-0130: Settlement batch overrun blocks early transactions

## Summary
Card payments failed for 48 minutes on the last business day of January while the settlement batch was still running.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV2
- **Date:** 2026-01-30, 06:30 to 07:18 (Asia/Colombo), 48 minutes
- **Affected services:** End-of-Day Settlement Batch (`eod-settlement-batch`), Ledger Database Cluster (`ledger-db`), Card Authorization Service (`card-auth-service`)
- **Customer impact:** 5,100 transactions failed or timed out; about 4,300 customers affected.
- **Incident commander:** Amaya de Silva

## Detection
Authorization timeout alert at 06:33.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 06:30 | Batch exceeds window; smaller commits now in place. |
| 06:33 | Authorization timeouts on accounts linked to the fee ledger. |
| 06:55 | Lock waits traced to the fee calculation step, which still runs as one transaction. |
| 07:10 | Fee step cancelled and rescheduled. |
| 07:18 | Recovery. |

## Root Cause
Commit size was reduced for postings, but the fee calculation step at the end of the batch still ran as a single database transaction across all merchant fee accounts. When it overlapped the morning peak, card authorizations that update the fee accounts waited on its locks and timed out.

## Contributing Factors
- The automatic window-end pause was still open from the previous incident.
- Fee step was not included in the commit-size change.

## Resolution
Fee step cancelled and rerun at 10:00.
