# INC-2026-0109: Delayed SMS one-time passwords

## Summary
SMS one-time passwords were delayed by up to 6 minutes for 90 minutes. Some customers abandoned online purchases while waiting for the OTP, but no payment system failed.

## Impact
- **Severity:** SEV3
- **Date:** 2026-01-09, 09:00 to 10:30 (Asia/Colombo), 90 minutes
- **Affected services:** Notification Service (`notification-service`)
- **Customer impact:** About 3,200 customers affected. No transactions failed.
- **Incident commander:** Lahiru Mendis

## Detection
OTP delivery latency alert at 09:08.

## Timeline
| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 09:00 | SMS aggregator starts throttling the Bank's sender ID. |
| 09:08 | Latency alert. |
| 09:40 | Traffic moved to secondary aggregator. |
| 10:30 | Backlog cleared. |

## Root Cause
The primary SMS aggregator applied a new per-sender rate limit without notice. Messages queued at the aggregator.

## Contributing Factors
- Failover to the secondary aggregator was manual.

## Resolution
Traffic moved to the secondary aggregator.
